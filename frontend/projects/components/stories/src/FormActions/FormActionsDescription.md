The Save / Cancel footer for forms and dialogs. `tar-form-actions` renders an optional `mat-button` Cancel and a `mat-flat-button` Save, and emits `saved` and `cancelled`. Save is disabled while the form is not `dirty`, while `saving`, or when `disabled`; Cancel is disabled while not `dirty` or while `saving`, and is omitted when `cancelLabel` is `null`. While `saving`, Save shows an 18px indeterminate `mat-progress-spinner`.

The container is `.tar-form-actions` with modifiers `.tar-form-actions--start`, `.tar-form-actions--space-between` (from `align`; `end` is the default) and `.tar-form-actions--sticky` (surface background, top border and `position: sticky; bottom: 0` on the inner div — see the Sticky story for its limits). The buttons are `.tar-form-actions__cancel` and `.tar-form-actions__save` with `data-testid` from `cancelTestId` / `saveTestId`; the spinner is `.tar-form-actions__spinner`. Save is `type="submit"` by default (`saveType`).
