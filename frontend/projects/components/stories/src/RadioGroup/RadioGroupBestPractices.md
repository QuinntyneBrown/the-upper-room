## Best practices

### Layout

- Stack options vertically; use `inline` only for two or three short options.
- Use a radio group for 2–5 options; more belong in a `tar-select`.

### Content

- Pre-select the most common or safest option when there is a sensible default.
- Keep labels parallel in form ("Email", "Phone", "Text message").

### Accessibility

- Give every group a `label` or `ariaLabel` — it names the group for screen readers.
- Arrow keys move between options; Tab moves into and out of the group as a single stop.
