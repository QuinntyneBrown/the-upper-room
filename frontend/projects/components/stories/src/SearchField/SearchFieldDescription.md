The search box for list screens. `tar-search-field` wraps an Angular Material `mat-form-field` (always `outline`, always full width) around a `type="search"` `matInput` with a leading search icon. It is controlled, not a `ControlValueAccessor`: pass `value` and listen to `valueChange` (every keystroke) and `searchKeydown` (raw `KeyboardEvent`s, e.g. for Enter or arrow keys). When `value` is non-empty a clear button appears; clicking it emits `valueChange` with `''` and then `cleared`. A public `focus()` method focuses the input.

The form field carries `.tar-search-field`; the icon is `.tar-search-field__icon`; the input is `.tar-search-field__input` with `data-testid` from `testId`, and `aria-label` falls back from `ariaLabel` to `label` to `placeholder`. The clear button is `.tar-search-field__clear` with `aria-label="Clear search"` and `data-testid="{testId}-clear"`. `placeholder` defaults to "Search" and `autocomplete` to `off`.
