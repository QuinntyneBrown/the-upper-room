A password input with a show/hide toggle. `tar-password-field` wraps an Angular Material `mat-form-field` (always `outline`) around a `matInput` whose `type` flips between `password` and `text`, with a `mat-icon-button` suffix that toggles it. Like `tar-text-field` it is controlled, not a `ControlValueAccessor`: pass `value`, listen to `valueChange` and `blurred`. `label` defaults to "Password" and `autocomplete` to `current-password`.

The form field carries `.tar-password-field` (plus `.tar-password-field--full-width`); the input is `.tar-password-field__input` with `data-testid` from `testId`; the toggle is `.tar-password-field__toggle` with `aria-label` "Show password" / "Hide password" and `data-testid` from `toggleTestId`, defaulting to `{testId}-toggle`. `hint` renders `.tar-password-field__hint`, and `error` renders outside the field as `<span class="tar-password-field__error" role="alert">` (`data-testid` from `errorTestId`, defaulting to `{testId}-error`).
