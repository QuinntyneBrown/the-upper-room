A single-line text input. `tar-text-field` wraps an Angular Material `mat-form-field` (`outline` or `fill` appearance, dynamic subscript sizing) around a `matInput`. It is a controlled, presentational field — not a `ControlValueAccessor`: pass `value` in and listen to `valueChange` (emitted on every `input` event with the raw string) and `blurred`.

The `mat-form-field` carries `.tar-text-field` (plus `.tar-text-field--full-width`, on by default); the `<input>` is `.tar-text-field__input` with `data-testid` from `testId`, and `aria-label` falls back to `label`. Optional `prefixIcon`/`suffixIcon` render `.tar-text-field__prefix` / `.tar-text-field__suffix`, and `hint` renders `.tar-text-field__hint` (`data-testid` from `hintTestId`). `error` is rendered **outside** the form field as `<span class="tar-text-field__error" role="alert">`, with `data-testid` from `errorTestId`, defaulting to `{testId}-error`.
