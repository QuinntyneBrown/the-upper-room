## Best practices

### Layout

- Render it in place of the list, inside the same container, so the page header and filters stay put.
- Offer at most two actions, with the primary one first.

### Content

- Heading says what's missing: "No partners yet", "No events match".
- Body says why or what to do next, in one sentence.
- Distinguish "nothing exists yet" (offer to add) from "nothing matches" (offer to clear filters).
- Pick an icon that matches the entity — `contacts`, `partners`, `events`, `kanban`.

### Accessibility

- The heading is an `h2`; make sure that fits the page's heading outline.
- The icon is decorative — never put meaning in the icon alone.
